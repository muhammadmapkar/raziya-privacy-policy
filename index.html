Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Raziya Adam Perfumes ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, and protects information in connection with our Instagram content automation tool ("the App"). The App uses the Meta Graph API to post content to our own Instagram business account on our behalf.

1. Information We Collect

  • Instagram Business Account Data: Basic account information (such as account ID and username) needed to authenticate with the Meta Graph API and publish content.
  • Images: Product and promotional images uploaded by us for posting to Instagram.
  • Captions and Text Content: Post captions, hashtags, and related text generated or entered for publishing.

We do not collect data from Instagram users who follow, like, or comment on our posts. We do not collect data from any Instagram account other than our own business account.

2. How We Use This Information

All collected data is used solely to operate the App, specifically to:

  • Authenticate with the Meta Graph API on behalf of our own Instagram business account.
  • Schedule and publish images and captions to our Instagram business account.
  • Maintain a record of content that has been posted or is scheduled to be posted.

3. No Selling or Sharing of Data

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share any collected data with third parties. Data is used exclusively for the internal operation of our own Instagram business account.

4. Data Storage

Content data (captions, image references, and posting schedules) is stored in Google Sheets that are owned and controlled solely by us. Access to this storage is restricted to the business owner. We do not use third-party databases or external storage services beyond Google's infrastructure, which we control directly.

5. Data Retention

Data is retained only as long as necessary to operate the App and maintain a record of posted content. We may delete records at our discretion.

6. Your Rights

Since this App operates on our own Instagram business account rather than collecting data from third-party users, there is no end-user data to request, correct, or delete. If you have questions about our data practices, you may contact us using the details below.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
